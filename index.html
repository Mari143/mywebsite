About Me

Engineer by training.
Dealmaker by design.

I'm Ammar — a Sales Engineer who gets genuinely excited by a well-structured PLC program and a beautifully timed commercial close. I studied Electrical & Electronic Engineering (BEng Hons) at the University of Huddersfield and the University of Hull — so I speak both "Engineer" and "Business", often in the same sentence.

My edge: I walk into a technical review, earn credibility with real engineering depth — robotics, vision systems, control architecture — then walk into a boardroom and turn that credibility into a signed contract. I've done it across industrial automation, renewable energy and emergency-response vehicles, selling to OEMs and end users across the UK, Europe, the Middle East and beyond.

Complex, multi-stakeholder capital-equipment cycles are my natural habitat. Map the buying committee. Build the business case. Survive three rounds of budget scrutiny. Win the line — and the service contract that follows it.
